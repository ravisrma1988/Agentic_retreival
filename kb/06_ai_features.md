# AI Features

## AI captions

AI captions automatically transcribe speech and add editable, styled captions to your video. AI captions support 32 languages and are available on Pro, Team and Enterprise. They are not included in the Free or Basic plans.

## Auto-reframe

Auto-reframe keeps the speaker or main subject in frame while converting a video to another aspect ratio. Supported aspect ratios are 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5. Auto-reframe is available on Pro and higher plans.

## Clip Finder

Clip Finder analyses a long video and suggests short clips suitable for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. Pro includes up to 100 Clip Finder clips per month, and Team and Enterprise include unlimited clips.

## AI voiceover

AI voiceover generates narration from a script in 12 languages, with a choice of 40 voices. It is available on Pro and higher plans.

## B-roll suggestions

AI B-roll suggestions recommend stock clips that match what is being said in your video. This feature is available on Pro and higher plans because it uses the stock video library.

## Use of your content for AI training

AutoStream does not use your videos to train AI models unless you explicitly opt in from Settings > Privacy.
