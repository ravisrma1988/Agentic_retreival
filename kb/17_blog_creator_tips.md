# Blog: Five Editing Tips for Busy Creators

## Plan your hook

The first three seconds decide whether viewers keep watching. Script a clear hook before you record.

## Cut the silence first

Run silence removal before any other edit. It shortens most talking-head videos noticeably and makes later trimming faster.

## Repurpose long videos

Turn each long video into several shorts. Tools such as Clip Finder help you spot the strongest moments.

## Keep captions on

Many viewers watch on mute, so captions improve retention on every platform.

## Batch your publishing

Edit several videos in one session and schedule them to go out across the week.
