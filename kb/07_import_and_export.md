# Import and Export

## Supported import formats

AutoStream imports video in MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI and WebM formats, audio in MP3, WAV and AAC formats, and images in PNG and JPG formats. You can also import files directly from Google Drive and Dropbox on all plans.

## Maximum upload file size

The maximum size of a single uploaded file is 1 GB on Free, 2 GB on Basic, 10 GB on Pro, and 20 GB on Team and Enterprise.

## Export formats

All plans can export MP4 (H.264 or H.265) and GIF. MOV export is available on Pro and higher plans. Apple ProRes export is available on Team and Enterprise only.

## Export resolution and frame rate

Free and Basic export at up to 720p and 30 frames per second. Pro, Team and Enterprise export at up to 4K and 60 frames per second. During the Pro free trial, exports are limited to 1080p.

## Export time

Most exports finish in less time than the length of the video. Long 4K exports can take longer, and you can close the browser while an export runs because rendering happens on AutoStream's servers.
