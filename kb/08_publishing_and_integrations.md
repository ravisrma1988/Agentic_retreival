# Publishing and Integrations

## Direct publishing

Pro, Team and Enterprise users can publish videos directly from AutoStream to YouTube, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn and X (Twitter). Free and Basic users download their exported videos and upload them to each platform manually.

## Scheduling

Scheduled publishing lets you choose a date and time for a video to go live on a connected platform. Scheduling is available on Pro and higher plans.

## Connecting a social account

Connect a platform from Settings > Connections. For security reasons, platform connections expire after 90 days and must be reconnected.

## Cloud storage integrations

Google Drive and Dropbox can be connected on all plans to import media.

## Zapier

The Zapier integration, which can trigger workflows when an export finishes, is available on Team and Enterprise.

## API

A public API is in private beta and is available only to Enterprise customers.
