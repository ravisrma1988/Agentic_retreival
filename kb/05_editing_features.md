# Editing Features

## Timeline editor

AutoStream includes a multi-track timeline editor with trimming, splitting, transitions, text overlays, colour presets and multi-track audio. The timeline editor is available on all plans.

## Silence removal

Auto-cut silence removal detects pauses in speech and removes them automatically. It is available on all plans, including Free.

## Filler-word removal

Filler-word removal detects words such as "um" and "uh" and removes them from the timeline. It is available on Pro, Team and Enterprise.

## Background noise removal

Background noise removal cleans up hum, wind and room noise in voice recordings. It is available on Basic and all higher plans.

## Stock media library

All plans include access to more than 10,000 royalty-free stock music tracks. The library of over 1 million stock video clips is available on Pro, Team and Enterprise.

## Templates

The Free plan includes 20 templates, Basic includes 100 templates, and Pro, Team and Enterprise include the full library of more than 1,000 templates.

## Version history

Pro keeps version history for 30 days, Team keeps it for 180 days, and Enterprise keeps it without a time limit. Free and Basic do not include version history.
