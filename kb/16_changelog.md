# Product Changelog

## March 2025: Pro price change

The Pro plan price changed from $69 per month to $79 per month for new subscribers. Customers who subscribed to Pro before March 2025 keep the $69 monthly price until March 2026.

## June 2025: AI voiceover launch

AI voiceover launched on Pro and higher plans with 12 languages.

## September 2025: More Team storage

Shared storage on the Team plan increased from 1 TB to 2 TB.

## January 2026: More caption languages

AI captions expanded from 20 languages to 32 languages.

## April 2026: Mobile publishing

The iOS and Android apps gained direct publishing to connected platforms.
