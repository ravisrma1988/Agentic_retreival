# Free Trial and Discounts

## Pro free trial

New users can try the Pro plan free for 14 days. No credit card is required to start the trial. The trial includes all Pro features, except that exports during the trial are limited to 1080p resolution. Each person or organisation can use the trial once. When the trial ends, the account moves to the Free plan unless you choose a paid plan.

## Student discount

Students get 50% off the Pro plan, on either monthly or annual billing. Eligibility is verified with a valid university email address, and the verification must be renewed every 12 months.

## Nonprofit discount

Registered nonprofit organisations get 30% off the Pro and Team plans. Apply by sending proof of nonprofit status to sales@autostream.example.

## Combining discounts

Discounts cannot be combined with each other or with other promotions. The annual billing saving can be combined with the student or nonprofit discount.
