# Troubleshooting

## Export stuck or failing

If an export has not progressed for more than 30 minutes, cancel it and start it again. Check that your account has enough free storage, because exports fail when storage is full.

## Upload fails

Uploads fail when a file is larger than your plan's maximum upload size or is in an unsupported format. Check the file against the limits in the Import and Export guide and make sure your internet connection is stable.

## Captions out of sync

If captions drift out of sync with the audio, open the Captions panel and click "Resync captions".

## Slow or choppy preview

Turn on Proxy mode in the preview settings to play a lower-resolution copy while editing. Proxy mode does not affect the final export quality.

## Social account disconnected

If publishing fails with an authorisation error, the platform connection has probably expired. Reconnect the platform from Settings > Connections.
