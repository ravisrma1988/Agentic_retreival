# Enterprise

## Who Enterprise is for

Enterprise is designed for media companies, agencies and brands with 20 or more editors. Pricing is custom and is agreed in an annual contract.

## Identity and access

Enterprise supports SAML single sign-on (SSO) and SCIM user provisioning, along with audit logs that are retained for 365 days.

## Reliability

Enterprise includes a 99.9% uptime service level agreement (SLA), with service credits if the SLA is missed.

## Data residency

Enterprise customers can choose to store their data in the United States, the European Union or India.

## Account management

Each Enterprise customer has a dedicated customer success manager and can use phone support. Critical issues receive a first response within 1 hour.

## Getting a quote

To get an Enterprise quote, email sales@autostream.example with the number of seats you need.
