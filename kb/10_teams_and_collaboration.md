# Teams and Collaboration

## Seats

The Team plan includes 5 seats. Additional seats can be added at $35 per seat per month from the admin console. Enterprise contracts start at 20 seats.

## Roles

The Team admin console has four roles: Owner, Admin, Editor and Viewer. Viewers can watch and comment on projects but cannot edit them.

## Review and approval

Team and Enterprise include review links with frame-accurate comments and an approval status for each video. Reviewers do not need an AutoStream account to leave comments on a review link.

## Shared storage and brand kits

Team workspaces share 2 TB of storage across all members and can create unlimited brand kits containing logos, fonts and colours. Pro includes one brand kit.

## Moving from Pro to Team

When a Pro user upgrades to Team, existing projects move into the shared team workspace automatically.
