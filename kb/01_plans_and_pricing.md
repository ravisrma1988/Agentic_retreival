# Plans and Pricing

AutoStream offers five plans: Free, Basic, Pro, Team and Enterprise. All prices are listed in US dollars (USD) and do not include taxes; applicable taxes such as GST or VAT are calculated at checkout based on your billing address.

## Free plan

The Free plan costs $0 and is intended for trying AutoStream. It allows 3 video exports per month at up to 720p resolution, a maximum video length of 5 minutes, and 5 GB of storage. Videos exported on the Free plan carry an AutoStream watermark. AI captions are not included.

## Basic plan

The Basic plan costs $29 per month, or $290 per year with annual billing. It includes 10 video exports per month, export resolution up to 720p, a maximum video length of 15 minutes, and 50 GB of storage. Exports have no watermark. AI captions are not included in Basic. Support is provided by email.

## Pro plan

The Pro plan costs $79 per month, or $790 per year with annual billing. It includes unlimited video exports, export resolution up to 4K, a maximum video length of 2 hours, and 500 GB of storage. Pro includes AI captions, auto-reframe, Clip Finder, AI voiceover, one brand kit, direct publishing and scheduling, and 24/7 live chat support.

## Team plan

The Team plan costs $199 per month, or $1,990 per year with annual billing, and includes 5 seats. Additional seats cost $35 per seat per month. Team includes everything in Pro plus 2 TB of shared storage, review and approval workflows, unlimited brand kits, ProRes export, an admin console with roles, and priority support.

## Enterprise plan

Enterprise pricing is custom and starts at a minimum of 20 seats. Contact sales@autostream.example for a quote. Enterprise adds SAML single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, a 99.9% uptime SLA, a dedicated customer success manager, data residency options and audit logs.

## Annual billing

Annual billing on Basic, Pro and Team gives you two months free compared with paying monthly, a saving of about 17%.
