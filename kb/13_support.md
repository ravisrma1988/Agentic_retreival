# Customer Support

## Support by plan

Free users can use the help centre and the community forum. Basic users get email support with a first response within 48 hours. Pro users get 24/7 live chat support. Team users get 24/7 priority support with a first response within 4 hours. Enterprise customers get a dedicated customer success manager, phone support, and a first response within 1 hour for critical issues.

## Contact addresses

General support: support@autostream.example. Sales and Enterprise enquiries: sales@autostream.example.

## Languages

Support is available in English and Hindi.
