# Refunds and Cancellation

## Refund policy for monthly plans

Monthly Basic, Pro and Team subscriptions can be refunded in full if you request the refund within 7 days of the first charge for that plan. Monthly renewal charges are not refundable.

## Refund policy for annual plans

Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, the remaining term of an annual plan is not refundable. Annual renewal charges can be refunded within 7 days of the renewal date. Enterprise refunds follow the terms of the customer's contract.

## How to request a refund

Request a refund from Settings > Billing > Request refund, or by emailing support@autostream.example. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing. There is no cancellation fee. After cancelling you keep access to your paid features until the end of the current billing period, after which the account moves to the Free plan.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Settings > Account > Delete account. All projects and media are permanently erased within 30 days of an account deletion request. Deleted individual projects stay in the Trash for 30 days before permanent removal.
