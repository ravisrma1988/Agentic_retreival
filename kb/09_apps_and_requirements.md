# Apps and System Requirements

## Web app

The web app runs in the latest versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. No installation is needed.

## Desktop app

Desktop apps are available for Windows 10 or later and macOS 12 or later. There is no Linux desktop app; Linux users can use the web app in a supported browser.

## Hardware requirements

AutoStream needs at least 8 GB of RAM. 16 GB of RAM is recommended for editing 4K video.

## Mobile app

Mobile apps are available for iOS 16 or later and Android 10 or later. The mobile apps support trimming, reviewing captions and publishing. Full timeline editing is available only in the web and desktop apps.

## Offline use

The desktop app can open and edit projects offline, but exporting, AI features and syncing require an internet connection.
