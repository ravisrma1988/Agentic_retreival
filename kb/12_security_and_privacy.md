# Security and Privacy

## Encryption

All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher.

## Compliance

AutoStream holds a SOC 2 Type II report and complies with the GDPR. A data processing agreement (DPA) is available for Team and Enterprise customers.

## Data hosting

By default, customer data is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) data centres in the United States. Enterprise customers can choose EU or India data residency.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available on all accounts, including Free, and can be enabled from Settings > Security.

## AI training

Your videos are not used to train AI models unless you opt in from Settings > Privacy.
