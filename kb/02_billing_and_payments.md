# Billing and Payments

## Accepted payment methods

AutoStream accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit or debit cards, PayPal, and UPI for customers in India. Annual Team and Enterprise subscriptions can also be paid by bank transfer against an invoice with net-30 payment terms.

## Billing date

Your billing date is the day you first subscribed to a paid plan. Monthly plans renew on the same day each month and annual plans renew on the same date each year.

## Invoices

Invoices can be downloaded from Settings > Billing > Invoices. Businesses in India can add their GSTIN in billing settings to receive a GST invoice.

## Upgrading and downgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately, and you receive a prorated credit for the unused part of your current plan. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, so you keep your current features until then. You can switch from monthly to annual billing at any time.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, AutoStream retries the charge 3 times over 7 days and notifies you by email each time. If all retries fail, the account is moved to the Free plan. Your projects are preserved, but projects above the Free storage limit become read-only until you upgrade again.
