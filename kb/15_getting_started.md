# Getting Started

## Creating an account

You can sign up with a Google account, an Apple ID, or an email address and password. New accounts start on the Free plan, and you can start the 14-day Pro trial at any time.

## Your first project

After signing up, create a workspace, upload a video or import one from Google Drive or Dropbox, pick a template, and edit on the timeline. When you are done, choose Export and select a resolution allowed by your plan.

## Recommended plan by creator type

Hobby creators who publish a few videos a month usually start with Free or Basic. Creators who publish more than 10 videos a month, need 4K, or want AI captions usually choose Pro. Agencies and teams with several editors usually choose Team.
